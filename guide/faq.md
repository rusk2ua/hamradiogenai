# Common questions

**"Doesn't AI just produce slop? It can't write real radio software."**
Ask a chatbot with no context and you'll often get exactly that. Give it the rules document, the datasheet and a sample log, build in small steps, and check the output against those sources, and you get tools you can trust on contest weekend. The difference is curation and verification.

**"Isn't this cheating? Real hams write their own code."**
Hams didn't stop building when we moved from tubes to microchips. AI handles the syntax and typing. The system design, the testing and the judgment about whether it's right are still yours.

**"Do I need to pay?"**
The free tiers are capable enough for a first project. Paid plans (roughly $20 a month at the entry level) make sense once you're running bigger multi-file projects or hitting usage limits. Check current pricing for your tool.

**"What about my private logs?"**
Work in a local folder and give the AI only the files the task needs. Be aware that whatever the AI reads is sent to the model to be processed, so check your account's data and privacy settings, and keep anything sensitive out of the folder. MCP servers you run yourself let you control exactly what the AI can reach; see [MCP safety](mcp.md).

**"I'm not a programmer. Can I really do this?"**
Yes. I'm not one either. Describe what you want the output to look like and where it should run, and let the model choose the libraries. Your job is knowing what "right" looks like.

**"Which tool should I start with?"**
See [the workbench](workbench.md). I use Claude for nearly everything now.
